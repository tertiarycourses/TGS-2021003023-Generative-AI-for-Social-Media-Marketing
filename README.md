## About the course

Learners plan and evaluate a social media marketing campaign, produce reviewed AI-assisted content, and use campaign evidence to decide what to improve. Practice uses a fictional Singapore SME and synthetic data.

## Learning outcomes

1. Develop a plan and objectives for a social marketing campaign.
2. Evaluate opportunities and the feasibility of social media platforms.
3. Create a generative AI social media campaign and monitor its outcome.
4. Evaluate AI campaign use and organisational impact.

## Topics covered

- Campaign objectives, audience evidence, customer journey, brand voice and economics.
- Platform feasibility, local relevance, privacy and pilot design.
- Captions, image and video briefs, creative tests, campaign hierarchy and content operations.
- Tracking, CTR/CPC/CPA/ROAS, fatigue, A/B interpretation, retargeting and impact review.
